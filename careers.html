Cleaning Technician

Three ways to work with us. Tell us on the application which ones interest you—you can choose more than one.

Part-Time

Scheduled shifts

A regular weekly schedule of roughly 15–28 hours, built around recurring residential and commercial clients. Best if you want predictable days and a steady route. Paid hourly, with mileage between job sites.

Flexible / On-Call

Work when you’re available

You tell us the days and hours you can take, and we send jobs that fit. Useful for Airbnb turnovers, last-minute deep cleans, and event work, which often lands on evenings and weekends. No minimum hours, but the more availability you share, the more we can send.

Independent Contractor

1099 · Per job

For experienced cleaners operating their own business. You accept jobs individually, set your own methods within our quality standards, and invoice per completed job. Requires your own transportation, general liability insurance, and a W-9.

What you’ll do

  • Clean residential homes, short-term rentals, offices, and event venues to our written checklist for each property
  • Dust, wipe, and sanitize kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, and living areas, including fixtures and accessible surfaces
  • Vacuum, sweep, and mop floors; remove trash and replace liners
  • Make beds, replace linens, and stage rooms so the space presents well for the next guest or client
  • Handle event work when scheduled: setting and resetting tables and chairs, clearing after guests, and returning venues to their original layout on deadline
  • Restock client-provided supplies and report damage, missing items, or maintenance concerns with photos
  • Use supplies and equipment safely, following product labels and client preferences (including eco-friendly options on request)
  • Communicate arrival, delays, and completion promptly, and treat every home and its contents with care and discretion

What we ask for

  • 18 years or older, and legally authorized to work in the United States
  • Reliable transportation to job sites across Northern Virginia
  • Dependable communication by phone or text, and consistent punctuality
  • A careful, detail-driven approach—prior cleaning experience is welcome but not required for part-time and on-call roles
  • Comfortable standing, bending, reaching, and lifting up to 25 lbs throughout a shift
  • Willingness to complete a background check before your first assignment
  • Professional, respectful conduct in occupied homes and businesses
  • Independent contractors: proven cleaning experience, own equipment and supplies, general liability insurance, and a completed W-9

Featured Service

Event Cleaning & Venue Reset

Weddings, receptions, corporate gatherings, church events, and private parties. We prepare the space before guests arrive, clear it after they leave, or handle both ends so the venue is returned exactly as it was found—on schedule and within your contracted deadline.

Pre-Event Setup & Venue Preparation

Before guests arrive
  • Full clean of event spaces, entryways, and guest restrooms
  • Table, chair, and seating arrangement per your floor plan
  • Linen placement, surface wipe-down, and final presentation check
  • Trash and recycling stations staged and lined
  • Walkthrough with your coordinator before doors open

Post-Event Breakdown & Venue Reset

After guests leave
  • Clearing tables, breakdown of chairs, tables, and staging
  • Trash and recycling removal to your venue’s designated area
  • Floors swept, mopped, or vacuumed; spills and spot stains treated
  • Restrooms and kitchen or prep areas reset
  • Room returned to its original layout before your reset deadline

Complete Event Package

Both services, one team
  • Everything in pre-event setup and post-event reset
  • One point of contact from setup through final walkthrough
  • Optional mid-event attendant for restrooms and trash
  • Coordinated to your venue’s access window and curfew
  • Simplest option when your contract requires same-night clearing

Event work is quoted by guest count, venue size, table and chair count, and how tight the reset window is. Share your details and we’ll confirm the crew size and timing. Late-night and same-night resets are available.
